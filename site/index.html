How to read this

Which interval is yours. City: heavy traffic, ride-hailing work and dusty village roads. Highway: long steady rides and touring. Change at the km shown or every 4 months, whichever comes first, and never past your bike maker's interval.

How the intervals are set. Semi-synthetic 2,500 km city and 3,500 km highway; synthetic 3,000 and 4,000 km. These are suggestions for Bangladesh's heat. Taka/km is the price of one litre divided by the interval.

Base oil and additives. Group III is hydrocracked "synthetic technology" oil; Shell's "PurePlus" is a gas-to-liquids (GTL) Group III base. * Group read from the maker's datasheet wording; † read from the maker's safety data sheet. TBN is the acid-neutralising reserve from detergent additives. Sulfated ash is the metal content of detergent and anti-wear additives; JASO MA2 allows up to 1.2%.
