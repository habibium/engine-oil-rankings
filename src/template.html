#Engine oil (10W-30, 1 L)
Greenbest in its columnRedworst in its column— not published by the maker≥ or > only a minimum is published, so it isn't ranked

Which interval is yours. City: Dhaka traffic, Pathao or Uber riding, and dusty village roads. Highway: long steady rides and touring. Change at the km shown or every 4 months, whichever comes first, and never past your bike maker's interval.

How the intervals are set. Semi-synthetic 2,500 km city and 3,500 km highway; synthetic 3,000 and 4,000 km. These are suggestions for Bangladesh's heat. Taka/km is the oil price divided by the interval.

Base oil and additives. Group III is hydrocracked "synthetic technology" oil; Shell's "PurePlus" is a gas-to-liquids (GTL) Group III base. * Group read from the maker's datasheet wording; † read from the maker's safety data sheet. TBN is the acid-neutralising reserve from detergent additives. Sulfated ash is the metal content of detergent and anti-wear additives; JASO MA2 allows up to 1.2%.

Prices and data. MRP or major Bangladeshi retailer price; shops often sell for less. AMSOIL is a seller's quote (৳1,750 for 946 ml) and its figures are from its 2018 datasheet. Pakelo and Motul 5100 figures are from current datasheets; stock here may be an older or regional version. JASO registration checked on JASO's own list of filed oils (1 Nov 2026).

Not included. Mineral oils. Pertamina and ELF: no 10W-30 motorcycle oil on sale here (Pertamina's Enduro Matic-V is a JASO MB scooter oil). Motorex: no current price. Essenza: no datasheet. 5W-30s: no gain in Bangladesh's heat, and most are car oils that can make a wet clutch slip.

Fake and refilled oil is common in Bangladesh. Buy from the official importer or a trusted shop, and check the seal before paying.
Sources: makers' technical and safety data sheets, AMSOIL data bulletin G3348, JASO filed-oil list (1 November 2026), Bangladesh Honda price list, Shell Bangladesh product catalogue, retailer listings (Rokomari, BikeBD, Daraz, Oil House, Sinopec Bangladesh) and a seller quote (AMSOIL).
